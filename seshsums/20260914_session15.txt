Work done this session:
- Created shiptrack/local_time.py: converts a lat/long position to local
  time at that location. Takes lat and lon as two decimal-degree CLI args
  and prints local time in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS) plus the UTC offset
  used, e.g. `python local_time.py 45.0 -91.9` -> "10:39:19 (UTC-6)".
  Per explicit instruction, uses the simple nautical convention (UTC
  offset = round(longitude / 15), clamped to [-12, 14]) rather than a real
  time zone database - no DST, no regional exceptions (Arizona, etc.).
  Validates lat in [-90,90] and lon in [-180,180], raising ValueError
  otherwise.
  - Verified with conda env "test": San Francisco (37.7749, -122.4194) ->
    UTC-8, London (51.5, 0.1) -> UTC+0, and (45.0, -91.9) -> UTC-6. Also
    confirmed an out-of-range latitude (-91.9 passed as lat) correctly
    raises a ValueError.
